I am an M.S. student at Pusan National University, working on agricultural robotics and sensing. My background in plant bioscience informs my interest in mobile environmental sensing and plant phenotyping. I expect to graduate in February 2027.

## Technical skills

- **Programming & data analysis:** Python, NumPy, pandas and SciPy; sensor time series, model fitting and visualisation.
- **Robotics & mobile sensing:** ROS 2, Unitree Go2, RTK-GNSS and LiDAR/IMU; environmental mapping and location-linked imaging.
- **Instrumentation & acquisition:** Force sensors, load cells, Raspberry Pi, Arduino and RS485; experimental and field data logging.
- **Imaging & phenotyping:** RGB/hyperspectral analysis, SAM, YOLOv8, Roboflow and ImageJ; fruit and soybean image analysis.
- **Finite-element modelling:** Abaqus/CAE and Abaqus/Standard; pile–soil contact and parametric uplift simulations.
