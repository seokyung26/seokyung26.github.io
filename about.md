- **Sensing:** RTK-GNSS, LiDAR/IMU, force sensors and load cells
- **Hardware:** Raspberry Pi, Arduino
- **Imaging:** RGB and hyperspectral image analysis, ImageJ
- **Simulation:** Abaqus/CAE, Abaqus/Standard

## Selected coursework

**Graduate:** Biological Image Processing; Biosystems Mechanics; Advanced Biomaterials Engineering I.

**Undergraduate:** Biosystems Control Engineering; Biosensor and Bio-Instrumentation & Practice; Plant Physiology and Practice.
