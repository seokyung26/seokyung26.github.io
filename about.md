<div class="about-intro"><p class="page-lead">From plant bioscience to agricultural robotics.</p><p>I am an M.S. student in Bio-Industrial Machinery Engineering at Pusan National University, based in the <a href="https://pass.pusan.ac.kr/">Precision Agriculture and Smart Sensing System Lab.</a> My expected graduation is February 2027.</p><p>My background in plant bioscience informs how I approach engineering questions: what can a physical measurement tell us about the crop, its environment or the structure supporting production? I work across robotic gripping, quadruped sensing, pile–soil interaction and structural instrumentation.</p><p>I plan to pursue doctoral research in agricultural robotics and sensing, with a growing interest in connecting mobile sensing to plant phenotyping.</p></div>
