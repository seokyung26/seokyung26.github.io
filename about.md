I am a master's student in the **Precision Agriculture and Smart Sensing System (PASS) Lab** at Pusan National University (Miryang campus), advised by Prof. Jaesung Park. I joined the lab in March 2025 after a B.S. in Plant Bioscience at the same university, where I first worked on image-based crop phenotyping and Arduino sensing.

My work sits between **agricultural robotics, sensing and structural analysis**:

- I turned a quadruped robot into a mobile sensing platform that maps greenhouse microclimate lane by lane and, for my thesis, maps soil-surface microtopography of sloping upland fields while treating every footstep as a small penetration test.
- I designed a force-sensing robotic gripper and showed that the *speed* of gripping, not only peak force, determines bruising in tomatoes. That result is now under review in *Biosystems Engineering*.
- For an air-dome vertical-farm project I run Abaqus finite-element studies of winged foundation piles, laboratory pull-out tests with digital image correlation, and an IoT monitoring system for anchor tension and tilt.

I enjoy building complete pipelines: hardware integration, ROS 2 / MQTT data acquisition, calibration, analysis and writing. I plan to continue to a Ph.D. in agricultural robotics and field sensing.
