# Seokyung Park (박서경)

**M.S. student · Precision Agriculture and Smart Sensing System (PASS) Lab**
Department of Bio-Industrial Machinery Engineering, Pusan National University
Advisor: Prof. Jaesung Park · [Lab website](https://pass.pusan.ac.kr/)

I build **sensing systems and robots for agriculture**: a quadruped robot that maps greenhouse microclimate and open-field soil surfaces, a force-sensing robotic gripper that harvests tomatoes without bruising them, and finite-element and field-monitoring tools for air-dome foundations. My background spans plant bioscience (B.S.) and bio-industrial machinery engineering (M.S.), so I like problems where crop biology, mechanics and data meet.

## Research interests
- Agricultural robotics: legged robots as mobile sensing platforms, robotic harvesting
- Field sensing: LiDAR-inertial SLAM, RTK-GNSS, force/tactile sensing, IMU, hyperspectral imaging
- Structural analysis for agricultural facilities: FEA (Abaqus), DIC, IoT structural-health monitoring
- Image-based plant phenotyping and deep learning

## Highlights
- First-author manuscript under review in *Biosystems Engineering* (robotic gripper speed control, 2026)
- Principal investigator, NRF Master's Research Grant (quadruped robots for Korean farmland, 2025–2026)
- Conference awards: KSAM 2024 & 2025, KSPA 2024 & 2025, PNU Practical Problem-Solving Research Group 2024
